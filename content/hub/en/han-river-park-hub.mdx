Stretching 40 kilometers across Seoul, the Han River (Hangang) is broken into 11 regional parks. Because the river is massive, walking between major parks takes over two hours. To avoid arriving at a concrete walkway with no convenience stores, you must pick a specific park based on exactly what you want to do: eat delivered chicken on the grass, watch a fountain show, or rent a bicycle.

## Which Park Should You Choose: Yeouido, Banpo, or Ttukseom?

- **How to get there:** Yeouido (Line 5, Yeouinaru Stn Exit 2, 2-min walk); Banpo (Lines 3/7/9, Express Bus Terminal Stn Exit 8-1, 15-min walk); Ttukseom (Line 7, Jayang Stn Exit 2, 1-min walk).
- **Recommended time to visit:** 4:00 PM to 8:30 PM. Arrive by 3:00 PM on weekends to secure a flat grass spot.
- **Estimated duration:** 3 to 4 hours.
- **Crowding & Precautions:** Saturdays at 5:00 PM hit peak capacity near Yeouido Delivery Zone 2. Always stay off the blue-painted bicycle paths when walking.

![Yeouido Hangang Park picnic spot with Seoul skyline and an easy riverside starting view](/images/hub/hangang-park/yeouido-hangang-park-picnic-spot-seoul-1-en.webp)

**Yeouido Hangang Park** is the primary picnic hub. Exiting Yeouinaru Station (Line 5, Exit 2 or 3) puts you exactly 50 meters from the riverbank. Dozens of rental kiosks outside the exit offer mat-and-table sets for around 15,000 KRW for 4 hours. It features three official Delivery Zones for easy food pickup.

**Banpo Hangang Park** is the undisputed night-view champion. Walk 800 meters (about 15 minutes) from Express Bus Terminal Station (Exit 8-1). At 7:30 PM, the 1,140-meter Banpo Bridge begins shooting illuminated water into the river. At 8:00 PM, the LED panels on the three Some Sevit floating islands light up. 

**Ttukseom Hangang Park** caters to active visitors. Just 20 meters from Jayang Station (Line 7, Exit 2), you can rent duck boats for 25,000 KRW per 30 minutes, or access the riverside swimming pool (5,000 KRW entry) during July and August.

## — Shade Tent Regulations and Fines

![Banpo Hangang Park night scene with Moonlight Rainbow Fountain and brighter riverside lighting](/images/hub/hangang-park/banpo-hangang-park-rainbow-fountain-night-2-en.webp)

Setting up a pop-up tent on the grass requires strict adherence to Seoul municipal laws. Park wardens patrol the lawns precisely at 7:00 PM.

- **Allowed Months and Hours:** April to October only. Hours are 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM (extended to 8:00 PM in June, July, and August). No tents are permitted from November to March.
- **Size and Setup:** Tents cannot exceed 2 meters by 2 meters. Crucially, at least two sides of the tent must remain unzipped and open at all times. 
- **Fines:** Violating the size, closing all doors, or pitching outside designated "Shade Tent Zones" incurs a 1,000,000 KRW fine. Normal open-air picnic mats (without a canopy) are legal anywhere on the grass year-round.

## How Do Food Delivery Zones and Ramen Machines Work?

Sampling hot chicken and ramyeon by the water is heavily structured due to the park’s size. Couriers cannot deliver to individual mats. 

At Yeouido, order your food to **Delivery Zone 2** (a 3-minute walk from Exit 2 toward Mapo Bridge). When your delivery app says the rider is 5 minutes away, stand directly under the numbered Zone 2 signpost. 

For the famous foil-tray instant ramen:
1. Go to the GS25 or CU convenience store 100 meters from the delivery zone. Purchase a 4,000 KRW ramen packet and foil tray. 
2. Go to the outdoor induction machines. Put the dry noodles in first, then the soup powder. 
3. Press "Cook Start". The machine pours boiling water and runs an exact 4-minute timer.
4. If you bought a raw egg (700 KRW), crack it into the tray when the digital timer shows 90 seconds remaining. Stir so it doesn't scorch the foil bottom.

## — Banpo Moonlight Rainbow Fountain Schedule

![Han River sunset view with people walking along the riverside path in Seoul](/images/hub/hangang-park/han-river-sunset-walking-trail-3-en.webp)

The Banpo Moonlight Rainbow Fountain operates from April through October. Each show lasts exactly 20 minutes.

- **Low Season (April–June, Sep–Oct):** 12:00 PM, 7:30 PM, 8:00 PM, 8:30 PM, 9:00 PM.
- **Peak Season (July–Aug):** 12:00 PM, 7:30 PM, 8:00 PM, 8:30 PM, 9:00 PM, 9:30 PM.

Sit on the concrete steps of Dalbit Square, 150 meters west of the bridge. Arrive by 6:50 PM to grab a clean spot before the 7:30 PM show. The fountain shuts down automatically if wind speeds hit 7 meters per second. 

## How Do You Rent a Bicycle at the Han River?

![Yeouido Hangang Park and Banpo Some Sevit night views compared for a Seoul riverside walk](/images/hub/hangang-park/yeouido-vs-banpo-hangang-park-night-view-4-en.webp)

The red and blue paved paths along the river are strict 20 km/h zones. 

Download the **'Seoul Bike (Ttareungyi)'** app. A 1-hour pass costs 1,000 KRW and accepts international credit cards. Alternatively, physical rental booths near the subway exits offer tandem (two-seater) bikes for 8,000 KRW per hour (requires leaving an ID card as a deposit).
